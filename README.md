# HumXus Demo - Humanoid Services On Demand

This is a demo and mock-up of a business model and idea for **HumXus** — Humanoid Services On Demand. From frontline hospitality to precision logistics and compassionate elderly care, HumXus provides trained humanoid assistants tailored to your needs.

## Concept

HumXus envisions a future where advanced humanoid robots can be deployed on-demand for a wide variety of applications:

- **Hospitality** - Front desk, concierge, and guest services
- **Foodservice** - Kitchen assistance, food preparation, and serving
- **Logistics** - Loading, unloading, and warehouse operations
- **Elderly Care** - Compassionate assistance and companionship
- **Entertainment** - Party hosting, variety shows, and events
- **General Assistance** - Household tasks and daily support
